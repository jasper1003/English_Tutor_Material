SECTION 2 · GRAMMAR REFERENCE

段考核心文法速查卡

1. 介系詞與必接動名詞 (V-ing)

after / before 後方若無主詞（作介系詞），動詞需加 -ing。

常見必接 V-ing 動詞/片語：give up, enjoy, practice, keep, finish。

2. 必接不定詞 (to V) 與否定位置

常見必接 to V 動詞：plan, decide, hope, want, need。

⚠️ 不定詞的否定：not 必須放在 to 前面（decide not to V）。

3. 語意大不同：stop & remember

  • stop to V：停下原本的事，去做另一件事。
  • stop V-ing：停止正在做的事。
  • remember to V：記得「去做」未做的事。
  • remember V-ing：記得「曾經做過」某事。

4. 固定片語與轉折副詞

save A from B（將 A 從 B 中拯救出來）。

however（然而）表語氣轉折，前後常有標點符號隔開。

SECTION 3 · READING PASSAGE

From the Farm to the Screen

Daniel grew up in a small village in southern Taiwan, where almost everyone in his family worked as a farmer. For decades, his parents woke up at five to five every morning to grow sweet pineapples and tea leaves. However, life was never easy for them. Because they didn't know how to sell their crops directly to big cities, they had to sell everything to middlemen at very low prices. Sometimes, insects even made tiny holes in the leaves, and buyers refused to take the fruit.

After graduating from college, Daniel got a job as a salesman at a busy tech company in Taipei. His boss was strict and always told the workers not to waste time during office hours. Daniel worked hard, but he often felt worried about his parents' farm back home. One evening, while he was watching clips online, he noticed that many young creators were becoming popular by sharing stories about their daily lives. Suddenly, a good idea came to his mind: why not use the internet to help his family?

The next weekend, Daniel went back to his hometown with a camera. He decided to make a video showing how carefully his parents took care of the land without using dangerous chemicals. In the video, he even showed a big spider spinning a web between two tea trees to prove how natural and healthy the farm environment was! After Daniel posted the clip online, thousands of people watched it and left warm comments. Many customers immediately ordered boxes of pineapples and tea leaves online. By connecting traditional farming with modern digital tools, Daniel successfully saved his family's farm from closing down.

While he was driving past an old factory, a small earthquake shook the ground, and falling rocks blocked the road. Instead of giving up, Mr. Chen stopped to check the fragile box to make sure it was still in good condition, and then he safely took another mountain path to finish his job.
