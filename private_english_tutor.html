Grammar Quick Reference Sheet

段考核心文法觀念速查表（Unit 4 動名詞、不定詞與轉折詞）

考點 1 介系詞與特定動詞後接 V-ing

  • 介系詞 + V-ing：after / before / without + V-ing
  • 必接 V-ing 動詞：give up / enjoy / practice / keep / finish + V-ing

考點 2 不定詞 to V 與否定位置

  • 必接 to V 動詞：plan / decide / want / hope / need + to V
  • 否定陷阱：不定詞的否定一律將 not 放在 to 前面 → decide not to V

考點 3 魔王辨析組：stop & remember

stop + to V vs. stop + V-ing

• stop to V：停下原本的事，去做另一件事（表目的）

• stop V-ing：停止正在做的這件事

remember + to V vs. remember + V-ing

• remember to V：記得要去做（尚未做）

• remember V-ing：記得曾經做過（已發生）

考點 4 固定片語與語意轉折副詞

  • save A from B：將 A 從 B 中拯救出來
  • however（然而，表語意轉折）vs. therefore（因此，表因果）

PART 2: READING Questions 9 – 12 | Read the passage and answer the questions below.
Reading Passage

From Traditional Fields to Online Screens: Daniel's Story

機考技巧：可用滑鼠反白文中關鍵句並點擊上方「螢光筆標記」，交卷後亦會自動標示出題依據。

A Daniel grew up in a small village in southern Taiwan, where almost everyone in his family worked as a farmer. For decades, his parents woke up at five to five every morning to grow sweet pineapples and tea leaves. However, life was never easy for them. Because they didn't know how to sell their crops directly to big cities, they had to sell everything to middlemen at very low prices. Sometimes, insects even made tiny holes in the leaves, and buyers refused to take the fruit.

B After graduating from college, Daniel got a job as a salesman at a busy tech company in Taipei. His boss was strict and always told the workers not to waste time during office hours. Daniel worked hard, but he often felt worried about his parents' farm back home. One evening, while he was watching clips online, he noticed that many young creators were becoming popular by sharing stories about their daily lives. Suddenly, a good idea came to his mind: why not use the internet to help his family?

C The next weekend, Daniel went back to his hometown with a camera. He decided to make a video showing how carefully his parents took care of the land without using dangerous chemicals. In the video, he even showed a big spider spinning a web between two tea trees to prove how natural and healthy the farm environment was! After Daniel posted the clip online, thousands of people watched it and left warm comments. Many customers immediately ordered boxes of pineapples and tea leaves online. By connecting traditional farming with modern digital tools, Daniel successfully saved his family's farm from closing down.
